## Shared mechanics used across games

- **Teams**: auto-balanced or host-picked; team color shown on each phone's top bar.
- **Turn order**: the app rotates fairly and highlights whose turn it is on the TV and vibrates that phone.
- **Night scoreboard**: every game maps its result to 0–1000 points so the ranking is fair across games of different lengths.
- **Awards at recap**: MVP, Best Liar (Imposter/Two Truths), Fastest Thumb (reaction games), Picasso (drawing votes), Comedian (Quip/Caption votes), Crowd Favorite (Most Likely To).
- **Skip and veto**: host can skip any prompt; any player can "veto" a dare once per night.
